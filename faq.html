Good To Know

Frequently asked questions.

The short answers to what most people ask before their first visit. Anything else, just call or email us.

What's the difference between black tie, formal, and cocktail attire?

Black tie means a tuxedo, full stop. Formal usually allows a well-fitted dark suit. Cocktail attire gives a little more room to be sharp without a full tuxedo. If your invitation doesn't say, tell us the venue and time of day when you start your order and we'll steer you the right way.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but an appointment gets you a dedicated fit specialist and no wait. We're open Monday through Saturday, appointment recommended.

Can I send my sizes before I come in?

Yes, that's exactly what Start Your Order is for. Enter your measurements or the brands you already wear, and we'll have your fit pulled and ready before you arrive.

I'm coordinating a wedding party. Where do I start?

Choose "For a Wedding Party" when you start your order. We'll take your details and your group's, then reach out to each groomsman directly to collect their sizes, so you're not chasing anyone down.

What's the $100 deposit for?

It confirms your order and holds your fitting date. The remaining balance is due at pickup.

What's the wedding party admin fee?

It's $50 to coordinate the group's fittings, and it's refunded in full once photos from the wedding are shared with Mr. Tux for our marketing and social media.

Do you offer alterations?

Every fitting includes in-house alterations if needed, done on site by our own team, not sent out.

I don't know my exact measurements. What now?

Tell us the brands you already wear instead, or skip sizing altogether. Either way, our fit specialists will take care of it in store.
