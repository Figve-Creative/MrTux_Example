Brand Story

The real story was never
in the inventory count.

Since 1977, Mr. Tux has been South Florida's longest-standing tuxedo and suit rental house: a distinction earned not through decades alone, but through a level of personal care that's become rare in formalwear.

Where It Started

Joe, Ani, and a dream.

Joe and Ani started Mr. Tux with a dream and a lot of hard work. Together, they built it into more than a rental shop: the largest in-house formalwear inventory in the area, trained fit specialists, and a level of care that turned first-time customers into regulars.

They are the soul of the place: the ones who remember a groom's name from his first fitting, and make a nervous teenager feel sharp before prom. Now their son, Jose, is carrying the legacy forward.
