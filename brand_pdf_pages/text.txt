                         Since 1977, Mr. Tux has been South Florida’s longest-standing tuxedo and suit rental house —
                         a distinction earned not through decades alone, but through a level of personal care that’s
                         become rare in formalwear. The store carries the largest in-house inventory in the area, so
                         customers try on the exact piece they’ll wear, fitted by trained specialists and backed by an
                         on-site tailor. Joe’s path began in the back room shining shoes, and through hard work he rose
                         to owning the business. Alongside Ani, he built Mr. Tux into more than a rental shop. They
                         are the soul of the place — the ones who remember a groom’s name from his first fitting and
                         make a nervous teenager feel sharp before prom. Now their son, Jose, is carrying the legacy
                         forward.
